What's new

0.13.29 · Oct 27

  • Assembly / cuts on a Pay Cashier slip are now added to LB Earn instead of Earning: 2 cuts on a 62" rescreen is $10.54 + $4.50 = $15.04. Other parts still go in Earning.
  • If the tag also has written-in extra labor, the cuts stay in Earning as before, with a note. The Earning note says when cuts went to LB Earn, so they're never entered twice.

0.13.28 · Oct 24

  • Patio screen door forms are read: always fiberglass, inches are width + height with each side rounded up (75½ x 48 = 76 + 48 = 124), and no build labor or $1.50 is added; Earning stays blank. Extras says "new patio door" and the size.
  • The handle height on a patio door form is never billed. If the size looks wrong, the inches are left blank and the row turns yellow.
  • The supplier's patio door order form (Circle Glass) isn't a tag; if it's scanned by mistake, nothing is added.
  • Two doors on one form ("Quantity 2"), or anything else unusual on a patio form, leaves a yellow row for you to check.

0.13.27 · Oct 24

  • Group tags also match on the phone number. When one tag has the full name and the others only a first name and initial, or a digit is misread, the tag still goes into its group's grey row instead of a new line.
  • When an order grows (a tag says "of 7" after the first said "of 6"), the new tags use the grey rows already saved, and one more row is saved for each extra tag. The Tag x of N cell gets a note to check the count.
  • If a later tag says a smaller count, no grey rows are freed; delete the note in column A to free a row you don't need.

0.13.26 · Oct 24

  • A tag year that only looks old (like 24 on a 2026 tag) no longer gets a note on the sheet; it is kept in the Scans tab. A missing date, or a date after the scan, still gets a note on Date in.
  • When the scanner isn't sure of a ticket because of another store, a Pay Cashier slip, a second screen or a rescan, the whole row turns yellow. If the ticket is just hard to read (Homer's price-gun labels included), only the ticket cell is coloured.
  • Group tags fill their own grey rows first, so two customers with the same last name don't take each other's grey rows. The same last name weeks later is no longer called a duplicate.
  • A row with your own note in column A is never turned grey. When a grey row is used, only the scanner's note is removed; anything you added stays, and so do your own colours on a row you typed in.
  • On a form with several frames, a balance-due slip isn't added automatically; the rows say to add it to the right frame by hand. A slip amount the scanner can't read turns the row yellow with a note to enter it.
  • Extra labor on a tag with a balance-due slip is left for you to check, so it isn't paid twice.
  • If a tag mentions a slip, a balance due, a wrong measurement or another screen, its inches are left for you to check, even when its numbers agree.
  • Extra labor and group parts are credited normally for very short last names (like "Li").

0.13.25 · Oct 23

  • A "measured wrong, balance due" slip adds its inches to the tag (for example 75 + 20 = 95), with a note to check.
  • Your own re-measurement written on a tag is ignored; the tag is billed as the customer was charged.
  • Price math written next to the rows (like 20 x .19) is no longer mistaken for a second size.

0.13.24

  • After a scan the phone says where it went ("Saved to Homer Glen, row 49") and, when something needs a look, what to check.
  • If an earlier tag didn't save, the phone names it and says how to clear the warning.

0.13.23

  • A group tag with a wrongly printed ticket still goes in its grey row; the ticket cell gets a note.
  • The note on grey rows is now plain words, without the code at the end.
  • Group tags scanned the same day still go together when the name or ticket is read a little wrong.
  • Tickets where a 7 was read as a 2 (or other look-alike digits) get a note with the likely ticket.
  • Homer's printed price-gun ticket labels are read as the ticket number instead of being skipped.

0.13.22

  • Group check: if one tag in a group has a different name, ticket or "x of N" from the others, that one cell gets a note. Nothing is changed for you.
  • Grey group rows still fill when the name is off by a letter.
  • Names like "Ann B" stay as written.
  • Fewer yellow rows for things that don't affect money (a crossed-out date, an overwritten "of 5").

0.13.21

  • Group tags stay together. When the first tag of a group (like 1 of 6) is scanned, the next rows turn grey and are saved for the rest of that group, in any order. Other tags skip the grey rows.
  • Saved rows free up after 14 days. To free one sooner, delete the note in column A.

0.13.20

  • Pins with no written quantity stay blank when prices are negative, conflicting or marked as refunds. These need a manual check.

0.13.19 · Oct 22

  • Everything on this page is now combined with the features already running: nightly workbook backups, a PDF of each month's Totals, and extra labor charges.
  • A pin slip that says "3rd pin" is left for you to check, since it means three pins in total.

0.13.18

  • Window labor is filled in: $18.50 a pane, $27 for thermopane. Panes come from the labor line, or one pane when the tag has one glass line.
  • Spring pins with no quantity written are counted from the price ($2.00 each), so their earning fills in.
  • A ticket with only the last 4 digits gets a note with the likely full 7-digit number.

0.13.17

  • The daily scan limit is now 300, so a big batch day won't be cut off.

0.13.16

  • Tag photos can be kept for 30 days in a private Drive folder, to help find patterns in misreads. A failed photo save never stops a scan.
  • This "What's new" page.

0.13.15

  • Fixed: correcting one parts earning by hand had stopped automatic parts earnings for the rest of that store's tab.
  • A ticket that doesn't fit the store's usual ticket numbers gets a note with the likely fix (never changed automatically).
  • A tag scanned twice with its ticket read differently is flagged as a possible duplicate.

0.13.14

  • Parts written on a Pay Cashier slip (like a frame spreader) are earned automatically.
  • A comment about one screen no longer holds back that tag's parts.

0.13.13

  • Far fewer yellow rows: only money or placement problems turn a row yellow. A doubt about one value (ticket, date, Tag x of N, name) tints just that cell. Phone-number doubts stay in the Scans tab.
  • Store tabs work whether they're named by store or by store number.

0.13.12

  • When the size and the QTY disagree, the $ totals or matching QTY rows can settle the inches, so prices fill in (still marked to check).

0.13.10 – 0.13.11

  • Tickets that aren't 7 digits are marked (a staple often hides a digit).
  • Handwritten parts lines under the printed list are read and earned.
  • Start new month creates the new file in the shared monthly folder.

0.13.8 – 0.13.9

  • Start new month can share the new file automatically.
  • The Scans tab stays at the end of the workbook.

Earlier

  • One-screen scanning page, remembered password, interrupted saves never duplicate a row, unplaced scans are counted on the phone, Date in from the tag and Date out from the scan.
